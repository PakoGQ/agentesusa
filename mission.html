The founder

Built by someone who's lived both worlds.

Francisco Gaitan was born in Los Angeles in 1984. He's spent his life moving between LA and Guadalajara — speaking English and Spanish natively, understanding both business cultures from the inside, running real businesses on both sides of the border.

Nexus exists because Francisco saw the gap firsthand: bilingual Latino professionals in the US — attorneys, accountants, logistics companies — are running their businesses with tools designed for someone else. The Fortune 500 has enterprise AI consultants. They have generic chatbots. We close that gap.

"My clients aren't customers. They're partners. We build for the long game — because if we do this right, we're building together for the next decade."

— Francisco Gaitan, Founder
